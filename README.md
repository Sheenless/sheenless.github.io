# mattheaton.info

Personal site for **Matt Heaton**, a research fellow working in agriculture at the interface of natural and social sciences. The site is built with [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com) (on a customised [Hyde](https://github.com/poole/hyde)/[Poole](https://github.com/poole/poole) theme) and is published via GitHub Pages at [mattheaton.info](https://mattheaton.info).

## Contents

- **Blog** (`_posts/`) — writing on food security, agricultural development, and sustainability.
- **Research** (`_research/`) — a Jekyll collection of research outputs, papers, and projects.
- **Data visualisation** (`_dataviz/`) — a Jekyll collection of visualisations exploring humanitarian and sustainability challenges.
- **Visual portfolio** (`_visual/`) — visual content and design work produced for research groups.

`research` and `dataviz` are declared as output collections in `_config.yml`, each with its own layout in `_layouts/`.

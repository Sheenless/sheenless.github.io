I supported the logo creation, graphic design and photography to support the BRIDGIT Project. 

[BRIDGIT](https://www.jic.ac.uk/brigit/) was a [12 organisation](https://www.jic.ac.uk/brigit/brigit-project-partners/) collaboration (led by the John Innes Centre) building the UK’s capability to prevent establishment of vector-borne plant pathogens and to increase its preparedness to respond should they be introduced. It's first target was _Xylella fastidiosa_, a bacterial disease limited to the xylem vessels of infected plants, described by the European Commission as “one of the most dangerous plant bacteria worldwide"

There were two factors that strongly influenced the design of the logo in the first stage:

1. The logo needed to show a mixture of plants, insects and microbes, to represent the range of expertise across the project.
2. The project had a strong citizen science component, so the logo should be particularly engaging for public and younger audiences.

We had a fantastic day with the UK-CGIAR Centre: Bringing together global expertise on gene editing regulation to inform alignment for sustainability, food and livelihood benefits (I'm behind the camera!). In short:

Gene editing regulations are rapidly changing around the world - You can [track these changes here](https://cropgeregulations.com/maps/) - Europe changed [just today](https://cropgeregulations.com/eu-council-adopts-new-ngt-regulation-for-gene-edited-crops/).

These changing regulations affect how how biotechnologies can be used as a tool to support sustainability, food and livelihood security. But these policies, and the systems through which regulations take place, are often sightly different in each country. 

Given the globalised nature of our food system and the overlaps in challenges new GE technologies could address, it is important to bring together policy and regulatory actors to learn from the opportunities and barriers experts face Doing so helps inform better regulations and their international alignment to unlock greater societal benefit from GE technologies.
